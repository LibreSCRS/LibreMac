# gate-job-exceptions.txt — gate steps that are deliberately NOT reached on push.
#
# Format:   <workflow>:<job>[:<step-number>]<two or more spaces><reason>
#
# workflow-step-order.py requires a row for every gate step whose job or step
# condition cannot be shown to hold on push. An empty reason fails, a row naming a
# job that no longer exists fails, and a row that excuses something needing no
# excuse fails — which is what makes these rows leave when the condition does.
